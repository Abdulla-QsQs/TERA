# Content and acceptable-use policy

TERA is a document-processing tool. It does not include exam-paper PDFs in this repository and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any examination board or source website.

Users must have permission or another lawful basis to access, transform, print, and distribute each source document. A file being freely reachable on a third-party website does not by itself prove permission to republish it. Follow the relevant examination board's terms, preserve required copyright notices, and use official institutional access where required.

TERA can omit explicitly blank question-paper pages, mark-scheme covers, and generic marking-guidance pages, and can place two marking pages on one printable sheet. Review every generated PDF before printing or sharing it. Do not use TERA to remove ownership notices, bypass access controls, publish answer material improperly, or misrepresent transformed documents as official editions.

Rights holders can request removal of a catalog source or integration by emailing `abdullahazam1077@gmail.com` with the subject `TERA rights request` and identifying the affected integration or URL. TERA aims to acknowledge a complete request within five working days and can disable a source while it is reviewed.
